Why Retail Leaders Are Joining

Retail is moving beyond traditional dashboards and static reporting. In 2026, growth will depend on how effectively brands turn fragmented customer, commerce, and engagement data into real-time retail intelligence.

This executive dinner is designed to bring together senior retail leaders for a candid conversation about:

How AI is reshaping retail decision-making

Where data analytics alone is no longer enough

Connecting digital insight to real-world customer experiences

Driving growth in an experiential economy

Set in an interactive cooking environment, this evening is built to encourage authentic discussion, practical insight, and meaningful peer connection.

An Executive Dinner Experience at Pinch of Salt

Rather than a traditional restaurant dinner, this evening is designed as a private, hands-on culinary experience.

Guests will gather in an exclusive, private space at A Pinch of Salt for a chef-led cooking class, where attendees will prepare a shared meal together before sitting down for a moderated peer discussion. The format is intentionally interactive, creating a more relaxed setting for conversation, collaboration, and connection.
