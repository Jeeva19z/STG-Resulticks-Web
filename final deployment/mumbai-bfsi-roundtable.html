This closed-door roundtable brings together senior leaders from mid-sized and emerging BFSIorganisations to exchange perspectives on how institutions are moving from customer data to real-world decisioning and measurable customer experience outcomes.

The discussion is designed as a peer-led conversation, focused on practical realities BFSIorganisations are navigating today rather than frameworks, presentations, or product demonstrations.

Context

As mid-sized and emerging BFSI organisations continue to invest in data and AI, many are navigating similar challenges:

Customer data that exists across teams and systems but remains difficult to activate

Personalization strategies that are hard to operationalise at scale

Increasing interest in AI for customer decisioning, alongside regulatory and governance considerations

Limited visibility into how customer experience initiatives translate into measurable outcomes

This roundtable creates space for an open discussion on how these challenges are being addressed in practice.

Discussion Focus

The conversation will centre on:

Customer data usability in BFSI and how fragmentation across systems and teams limits analytics and AI-driven decisioning

Personalization in an AI-influenced environment including the shift beyond rule-based segmentation and what “personalized experiences” means today

AI-led customer decisioning covering where decisions remain manual or campaign-driven

Executing AI-informed decisions across channels operational challenges of turning insights into customer action

Measuring CX impact and attribution focusing on the metrics that matter and evidence-based measurement

Participants

The roundtable is intended for a small group of senior leaders from mid-sized and emerging BFSI brands, including:

  • Digital, Customer Experience, Marketing, leaders who directly own customer engagement, personalization, and lifecycle strategies
  • Business and functional executives responsible for customer strategy, growth, and experience-led decision-making
  • Technology and data leaders who balance innovation in AI and data with regulatory, operational, and scale constraints

Participation will be limited to ensure depth of discussion and meaningful exchange.
