Why This Conversation Matters

Credit unions are operating in an environment where member expectations are increasingly shaped by large banks and digital-first financial providers. Convenience, personalization, and immediacy are now baseline expectations, yet trust, community, focus, and cooperative values remain core differentiators.

This roundtable is designed to create space for candid, peer-level discussions around a shared question: How do credit unions compete effectively with banks without losing the trust, identity, and values that differentiate them?

What to Expect:

Candid discussion on competing with banks without losing member trust or identity

Peer examples of growth, digital engagement, and differentiation

Clear takeaways on where technology helps and where it hurts
