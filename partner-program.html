Why partner with Resulticks

Joint branding and selling

Enjoy co-branding privileges for wide-ranging marketing collateral as well as online and offline events. Leverage expert assistance for marketing, sales, and post-sales activities.

Co-marketing budget

Qualify for marketing funding from Resulticks to help you develop, expand, and extend productive pipelines.

Resources for success

Tap into a wealth of training materials, product brochures, data sheets, proven email templates, competitor analyses, sales decks, and more to easily generate new business.

Access RESUL demos and live instances

Master the platform through exclusive demo videos and live instances that help you demonstrate the full capabilities and power of RESUL to new business leads.

RESUL certification

Access extensive RESUL training and certification programs to enable your sales, technical, and campaign personnel to develop expertise on critical aspects of the platform.

Dedicated partner manager

Enjoy the support of a partner manager to help you achieve your sales target. Beyond assistance with account and sales engagement plans, you also benefit from regular business reviews and strategic workshops to improve co-selling efforts.
