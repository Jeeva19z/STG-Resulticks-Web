AI That Creates With You

Five AI capabilities built into the creative workflow.

  • Full EDM generation from a prompt

    Describe the campaign and Genie generates a complete email with layout, copy, and images. Edit individual elements with spot prompts for headlines, body, or visuals.

  • Design and image generation

    Generate brand-aligned images and design variations from a text prompt. Create social media creatives tailored to specific channels with batch download.

    Generate different content versions for different audience segments within a single campaign. Each group receives copy and visuals matched to their profile and behaviour.
