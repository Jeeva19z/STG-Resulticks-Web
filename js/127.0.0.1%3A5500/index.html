The Intelligence Age Blueprint

Six reasons why this book is essential for every executive

You need a clear starting point — not more jargon.
57% of executives admit they lack confidence in their ability to understand AI's implications. This book gives you a plain-language, 12-step blueprint that moves you from uncertainty to decisive action — with a First 100 Days Action Plan you can deploy immediately.
You need to escape pilot purgatory for good.
Most AI initiatives fail not because of technology, but because of culture, mindset, and leadership gaps. This book diagnoses exactly where organisations stall and gives you the organisational readiness framework to break through and scale — sustainably.
You need a new blueprint for the C-suite itself.
The roles of CEO, CFO, and CHRO are being fundamentally rewritten. This book maps the new mandate for each — from orchestrating AI agents and gig ecosystems to shifting from historical accounting to predictive value creation. Stay ahead of the restructure.
You need more than a strategy — you need a philosophy.
Drawing from the Upanishads, Stoicism, and Sun Tzu, this book builds the inner operating system that no algorithm can replicate — the wisdom to make sound judgements under pressure, inspire trust in uncertain times, and lead from a place of clarity rather than fear.
You need a practical workforce transformation model.
The AI Skill Pyramid gives you concrete numbers for departmental planning — 100% foundational literacy, 20–30% practitioner capability, 10–15% specialist depth. No guesswork. No consultancy fees. A ready-to-use framework for upskilling at every level of your organisation.
You need to demonstrate ROI — not just intent.
This book goes beyond vision to accountability. Five distinct metric categories — Model Performance, Employee Engagement, Customer Satisfaction, Operational Efficiency, and Financial Impact — give you the measurement language to defend, justify, and accelerate your AI investment at every level.
